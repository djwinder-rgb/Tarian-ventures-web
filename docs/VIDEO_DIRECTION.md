# Original motion and future film direction

20 September 2026. These are creative briefs, not claims about Tarian's assets or operations. No external video service has been used, no paid generation commissioned and no third-party footage copied.

## Implemented: connected infrastructure

An original SVG composition of connected planes, sparse junctions and moving signal traces. Charcoal provides depth; pale structural lines and white signals establish hierarchy. An 18-second CSS loop changes only decorative strokes. The diagram does not depict an actual facility, network or physical asset.

The scene stays static until JavaScript enables motion. A visible Pause/Play animation button controls it; reduced-motion preferences disable it, including changes made while the page is open. No saved preference, third-party request, audio, stock image, video codec or animation library. The supplied shield remains the identity in the header/footer, not the decorative artwork.

### Image-to-video prompt

Animate the supplied Cardiff Bay photograph as a restrained architectural film for an independent Welsh venture-development website. Preserve the reference's actual buildings, shoreline, proportions and perspective. One slow, stable forward camera movement with minimal parallax; calm water and subtle natural atmospheric movement. Muted daylight, charcoal shadows, neutral highlights and understated colour. Compose for a wide website hero with uncluttered space on the left for typography added separately in HTML. No lettering, logos, people in close-up, fabricated data centres, new buildings, light trails, neon effects or dramatic transitions. Silent, 8–12 seconds. Begin and finish with a calm hold suitable for editing into a seamless loop.

Broad water, urban geometry and connected spaces; elegant, restrained and grounded. Slow forward movement, subdued daylight, charcoal and neutral tones with subtle green accents. No claim to depict a specific real site. No logos, readable signage, captions, identifiable people, science-fiction elements, neon networks or invented infrastructure projects. Wide 16:9 composition, negative space on the left, silent 8–12 second shot.

## Integration contract

Keep website text, links and controls in HTML. Start with a strong poster image; reserve the scene's dimensions to avoid layout movement. Use muted inline playback with a visible pause control and a still fallback for reduced motion or unavailable playback. Load no external player automatically. Review file weight and mobile cropping before selecting a final encoding. Background footage must not be the only source of information.

## Selection criteria

Compare each candidate inside the same hero layout. Does it add presence without competing with the message? Is geography honest? Are lines/buildings stable throughout? Is the loop seam unobtrusive? Does a mobile crop still work? Is the first frame strong without playback? Verify the chosen output's terms rather than assuming AI generation is rights-free.
