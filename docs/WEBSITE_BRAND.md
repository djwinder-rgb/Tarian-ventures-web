# Authoritative website brand

Approved by human visual review; formalised 19 September 2026. This document supersedes historical website palette and logo-presentation instructions. Approved architecture, copy, typography hierarchy, page compositions and spacing are frozen unless separately authorised.

- Forest `#13564D`: primary identity, major headings, selected controls, geometry and deliberate high-impact sections.
- White `#FFFFFF`: dominant canvas and inverse typography.
- Pale Forest `#F3F7F6`: subtle section differentiation; the approved footer also retains this colour for secondary text.
- Deep Ink `#12262B`: primary/supporting prose where it improves hierarchy and readability.
- Decorative Rule `#D0DDDB`: structural separators and non-essential geometry.

Midnight, Slate and Stone are superseded as general website tokens. Colours embedded in supplied authoritative SVGs are preserved unchanged and do not expand this website palette.

Desktop header: supplied horizontal lockup, unchanged. At 768px and below: supplied green shield in a 64px frame plus readable 16px system-font TARIAN text. The home link has the full accessible name “Tarian Ventures — home”; added visible lettering is hidden from assistive technology to avoid duplication. The full wordmark is not squeezed into mobile width. About uses the green mark; the footer uses the white mark. Master SVG artwork is unchanged.

The existing decorative desktop hero contour is centred within its original column at 80% width and 50% opacity. Its paths and stroke definitions remain unchanged. It is non-informational, hidden from assistive technology, and absent below the existing mobile breakpoint. Do not use reduced contrast for meaningful controls or content.

Forest/White contrast is 8.52:1; Ink/White 15.70:1; Ink/Pale Forest 14.54:1; Forest/Pale Forest 7.89:1. Keep visible focus, textual status and existing navigation behaviour. No photography or new visual concepts are authorised.
