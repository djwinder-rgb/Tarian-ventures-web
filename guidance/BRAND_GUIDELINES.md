# Tarian Ventures Brand System v1.0

## Positioning
**Building businesses around problems worth solving.**

Tarian Ventures is an independent venture development company. The identity should feel focused, credible, pragmatic, forward-looking and resilient.

## Logo
The core mark is a geometric shield incorporating a `T`, reflecting the Welsh meaning of *Tarian* (shield) without using literal national symbols.

Use the horizontal logo by default. Use the stacked logo where horizontal space is constrained. Use the shield alone for favicons, app icons and small brand marks.

Do not stretch, rotate, add shadows, gradients or unapproved colours. Maintain clear space around the mark equal to at least one quarter of the shield width.

## Colour palette
- Midnight `#101820` — primary brand colour; typography and dark surfaces.
- Slate `#35434A` — secondary text and supporting UI.
- Tarian Green `#1F6B57` — accent; use selectively.
- Stone `#F3F1EB` — warm primary background.
- White `#FFFFFF` — whitespace and reversed typography.

## Typography
Primary: **Inter**. Fallback: Arial, sans-serif.
The website implementation should self-host the selected font files under their applicable licence or use a system-font fallback. Do not load fonts from third-party CDNs.

## Imagery and provenance
V1 uses no third-party stock photography. Visual interest should come from typography, whitespace, the shield geometry, CSS/SVG line work and Tarian-owned assets.

Every non-code visual asset added later must have its provenance and licence recorded in `ASSET_PROVENANCE.md`.

## Tone
Precise, understated and evidence-led. Avoid unsupported superlatives such as “leading”, “world-class”, “revolutionary” and “cutting-edge”.
